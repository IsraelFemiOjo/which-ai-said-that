# Starter prompts: your first 7 rounds

Five prompts per day. Each one asks for a short answer so it fits a phone screen. Run every prompt in all four apps, then pick the answer from each that you think makes the best round. Mix the AIs across the five questions.

Tip: the best questions are the ones where the answers feel different. Opinions, jokes, advice and short creative tasks show personality. Pure facts all sound the same.

## Day 1: Hot takes
1. Is a hot dog a sandwich? Answer in under 60 words.
2. Is cereal a soup? Answer in under 60 words.
3. Pineapple on pizza: yes or no? Defend it in under 60 words.
4. Is it rude to leave someone on read? Answer in under 60 words.
5. Are morning people just better at pretending? Answer in under 60 words.

## Day 2: Advice
1. Give me one tip to stop procrastinating.
2. How do I tell my friend their business idea is bad? Keep it under 80 words.
3. I have 20 minutes before an interview. What should I do? Under 80 words.
4. How do I stop checking my phone first thing in the morning? Under 60 words.
5. My landlord raised rent again. What should I say to him? Under 80 words.

## Day 3: Roast me
1. Roast my plan to wake up at 5am every day.
2. Roast people who say "let's circle back".
3. Roast my 47 open browser tabs.
4. Roast someone who replies "k" to a long message.
5. Roast the idea of a gym membership in January.

## Day 4: Explain it simply
1. Explain why the sky is blue in two sentences.
2. Explain inflation to a 10 year old in under 60 words.
3. What is a black hole? Two sentences.
4. Why do onions make you cry? Under 50 words.
5. Explain how a credit score works in under 60 words.

## Day 5: Tiny creative jobs
1. Write a one line tagline for a small bakery in Lagos.
2. Write a four line poem about Monday.
3. Name a new energy drink. Give one name only and one sentence why.
4. Write a text apologizing for missing a birthday party. Under 50 words.
5. Describe rain to someone who has never seen it. Two sentences.

## Day 6: Would you rather
1. Would you rather be able to fly or be invisible? Pick one and explain in under 50 words.
2. Would you rather never use social media again or never watch a movie again? Under 50 words.
3. If you could delete one app from every phone on earth, which one? Under 50 words.
4. Pick one: always 10 minutes late or always 20 minutes early. Under 50 words.
5. If you had to eat one meal forever, what would it be? Under 50 words.

## Day 7: About itself
1. What is something you're bad at? Under 60 words.
2. Tell me a joke you think is actually funny.
3. What would you do with a day off? Under 60 words.
4. Describe yourself in one sentence.
5. What's a question you wish people asked you more? Under 50 words.
