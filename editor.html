Make a round

Paste the answers exactly as each AI gave them. Download the file, then upload it to the rounds folder on GitHub.

Keep it fair, every time
  • New chat for every prompt. Memory and custom instructions off.
  • Use each app's copy button so formatting comes across as is.
  • Never edit an answer. Not even a typo.
  • Write down the exact model or access tier shown in the app. It goes in your private record only. Players just see ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok.
  • Short prompts work best. Ask for under 80 words so answers fit a phone screen.
